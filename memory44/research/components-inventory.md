# memory44 — Base Game Component Inventory

Research notes for the original project. All counts below were cross-checked against the
manufacturer's published box-content sheet, retailer listings, and fan inventories.
This document refers to the classic (2004-era) printing of the base game; a short note on
the revised 2025-era edition appears at the end.

## Game Board

- One large, folding, double-sided board printed with a hexagonal grid of **13 columns × 9 rows** (117 hexes).
- **Side A — Countryside:** open green farmland/grassland with subtle texture, used for inland battles.
- **Side B — Beach landing:** a band of open water along one long edge, a strip of sand, then scrub/grass inland; used for amphibious assault scenarios.
- Both sides are split into three battle sections by two dotted divider lines running across the board: left flank (4 columns wide), center (5 columns wide), right flank (4 columns wide). The section division is central to how command cards work.
- The printed board alone is deliberately plain; all scenario-specific terrain comes from overlay tiles.

## Plastic Miniatures — 144 figures total

Soft, slightly flexible plastic in a small toy-soldier scale (roughly 1:72 / ~20 mm for infantry). Two armies of 72 pieces each, distinguished only by color: **Allies in olive green, Axis in blue-grey**. The official per-army breakdown:

| Unit type | Pieces per army | Pieces total | Figures per game unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infantry figures (single identical pose per nation) | 42 | 84 | 4 |
| Tank models | 24 | 48 | 3 |
| Artillery guns (gun + crew molded as one piece) | 6 | 12 | 2 |
| **Total per army** | **72** | **144** | |

So a full-strength infantry unit is 4 figures standing on one hex, an armor unit is 3 tank models on one hex, and an artillery unit is 2 gun pieces on one hex. Figures are removed one at a time as the unit takes hits.

## Command Cards — 60 cards

Standard playing-card size. Two families: **40 Section cards** (which board section may be ordered, and how many units) and **20 Tactic cards** (special moves or combat effects).

### Section cards (40)

| Card | Left flank | Center | Right flank | Orders given |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assault | 2 | 2 | 2 | All units in the named section |
| Attack | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 units in the named section |
| Probe | 4 | 5 | 4 | 2 units in the named section |
| Recon | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 unit in the named section |
| Recon in Force | — | — | — | 3 cards; 1 unit in each of the three sections |
| Pincer Move | — | — | — | 1 card; 2 units on the left and 2 on the right |
| General Advance | — | — | — | 1 card; orders spread across the whole front |

Total: 2+2+2 + 3+4+3 + 4+5+4 + 2+2+2 + 3 + 1 + 1 = **40**.

### Tactic cards (20)

Pairs with two different illustrations (2 copies each): Armor Assault, Counter-Attack, Direct from HQ, Infantry Assault, Move Out!
Single copies (1 each): Air Power, Ambush, Artillery Bombard, Barrage, Behind Enemy Lines, Close Assault, Dig-In, Firefight, Medics & Mechanics, Their Finest Hour.

## Battle Dice — 8

Eight custom wooden dice. Each die has six symbol faces instead of pips: **2× infantry figure, 1× tank, 1× grenade, 1× star, 1× flag**. Infantry/tank/grenade symbols score hits against the matching target type (grenades hit anything), stars resolve special effects, and flags force retreats. Symbols are burned/engraved into the wood.

## Terrain Hex Tiles — 44 double-sided tiles

Thick punchboard hexes sized to overlay the board grid. The manufacturer's content sheet lists the exact two-sided pairings:

| Count | Front / Back |
|---|---|
| 11 | Forest / Hill |
| 11 | Forest / Straight River |
| 4 | Forest / Town |
| 1 | Forest / Hedgerow |
| 8 | Hill / Hedgerow |
| 1 | Hill / Curved River |
| 2 | Town / Straight River |
| 3 | Town / Curved River |
| 1 | Hedgerow / Straight River |
| 2 | Hedgerow / Curved River |
| **44** | |

So the four basic terrain families in the base game are **forests, hills, towns/villages, hedgerows, and rivers** (rivers split into straight and curved segments). Rivers are impassable except at bridges; other terrain modifies movement and combat dice.

## Obstacles — 36 tokens + 4 bunker/bridge pieces

Rectangular punchboard tokens laid on hexes:

- **12 hedgehogs** (steel anti-tank obstacle beams) — 6 in each army's color.
- **12 sandbag** emplacements — defensive cover.
- **12 barbed wire** strands — 6 in each army's color.
- **4 double-sided punchboard pieces showing a bunker on one face and a bridge on the other**, used for fortifications and for crossing rivers.

## Tokens and Badges

- **10 double-sided victory medals** — round star-shaped medal tokens with an Allied face and an Axis face; used to mark eliminated units and objective hexes.
- **14 double-sided special-forces badge tokens** placed next to units to grant elite abilities. The pairings are fixed combinations of three elite unit types (an Axis elite division, US Rangers, and Free French resistance): 3 of Axis/Rangers, 3 of Axis/paratrooper-bridge assault, 2 of Axis/resistance, 3 of Rangers/paratrooper, 2 of Rangers/resistance, 1 of paratrooper/resistance.

## Cards, Booklets, and Storage

- **9 terrain/unit summary cards** (one each: beach, forest, hedgerow, hill, obstacle, ocean, river, town, unit) — quick-reference for movement and combat effects of each terrain type.
- **1 rules booklet** (core rules, movement, combat, terrain effects).
- **1 scenario booklet with 17 historical scenarios**, each with a full-color setup map, troop placement, special rules, and medal objectives. Scenarios cover famous 1944 battles such as the Pegasus Bridge coup-de-main and the Normandy beach landings.
- **Storage insert:** a vacuum-formed plastic tray filling the box, with separate wells sized for each army's infantry, tanks, and artillery, plus compartments for dice, tokens, and tiles and slots for the card decks; the booklets and folded board sit on top of the tray. Cardboard counter sheets must be punched on first opening.

## Note on the 2025-era revised edition

A refreshed edition of the base game was released in 2025 with the same game content but revised components: plastic obstacle miniatures instead of punchboard tokens, plastic (rather than wooden) dice, 22 summary cards instead of 9, 10 double-sided medal tokens, 14 special-forces badges, 4 player card holders, and 6 activation tokens. Some retailer listings mix the two editions' counts (e.g. "22 summary cards" vs "9 summary cards") — the 144 miniatures / 60 cards / 44 tiles / 8 dice / 36+4 obstacles figures are consistent across both.
