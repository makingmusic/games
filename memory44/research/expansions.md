# memory44 — Official Expansions

Functional summary of every official expansion for memory44, in release order.
All prose is original; unit and equipment names are historical hardware designations only.
Release years were cross-checked against publisher announcements and encyclopedic listings.

## Terrain Pack (2005)

The first expansion, focused entirely on map variety rather than new armies. Adds 66 new double-sided terrain hexes (roads that speed movement, railroads with a train, marshes, mountains, deserts, lakes, pontoon and railroad bridges, and more), 22 landmark tiles (airfields, radar stations, supply depots, barracks, factories, churches, lighthouses, dams), 20 round markers (minefields, medals), 16 obstacle tokens, and 28 special-unit badges (airborne, engineers, and similar elites). Includes rules covering these features plus North African desert warfare, and 4 scenarios. Its main purpose is enabling a much wider range of player-designed scenarios.

## Eastern Front (2005)

The first "army pack," introducing the Soviet Union as a playable nation with 42 infantry, 24 T-34 medium tanks, and 6 ZIS-3 field guns in a new army color, plus a special political-commissar command mechanic. Adds 44 double-sided winter-leaning terrain tiles (snowy forests, frozen rivers, ravines, villages), 10 round markers, 4 obstacles (including anti-tank dragon's teeth), and 14 special-unit badges. Contains 8 historical scenarios from the Russo-German war (including a Kursk scenario for the large-scale Overlord format and one from the Winter War against Finland) plus new national rules.

## Winter/Desert Board Map (2005)

A single additional double-sided folding board: one face shows a snow-covered winter battlefield, the other a sandy desert battlefield, so Eastern Front and Mediterranean scenarios look right. Bundled with simple campaign rules for linking scenarios together and "Blitz" rules adapting the game to early-war (1939–42) battles.

## Pacific Theater (2006)

Second army pack, introducing the Imperial Japanese army: 48 infantry, 12 Type 95 Ha-Go light tanks, and 6 Type 88 75 mm anti-aircraft guns. Adds 44 jungle-and-island terrain tiles (jungles, rice paddies, caves, rope bridges, coastlines), warship pieces (a destroyer and an aircraft carrier) usable on ocean hexes, a night-visibility chart governing fighting in darkness, and distinctive national rules (the Japanese "Banzai" charge, US Marines' rally ability, flame-throwing tanks, mobile artillery). 10 round markers, 4 obstacles, 14 special-unit badges (US Marines, British Chindits, Japanese special forces), and 8 historical scenarios such as Wake Island, Guadalcanal, and Guam.

## Air Pack (2007)

Introduces aircraft as a new unit category. Contains 8 pre-painted airplane miniatures covering the major powers (US: P-38 Lightning, P-40 Warhawk, F4U Corsair; UK: Spitfire; USSR: Yak-1; Germany: Bf 109 and Fi 156 Storch; Japan: A6M Zero), flight stands, an air-rules booklet with mechanics for ground attack and air superiority, new airplane summary cards, updated command cards integrating air power, updated versions of earlier scenarios, and some extra cardboard (bomb craters, exit markers, winter bunkers). The air rules were widely considered complex; the pack was discontinued in 2013 and functionally replaced by New Flight Plan (below).

## Mediterranean Theater (2008)

Third army pack, introducing the British Commonwealth forces: 42 infantry, 24 Crusader tanks, 6 25-pounder gun-howitzers, and 3 anti-tank special-weapon assets. Adds 44 desert terrain tiles (escarpments, ridges, ergs, wadis, oases, coastlines, desert airstrips, HQ and supply tents), 10 round markers (British medals, minefields, exit markers), 4 obstacles (desert field bunkers, roadblocks), and 14 badges (SAS, Royal Engineers, Italian units). New rules include British "stiff upper lip" resilience and Italian army quirks, plus the debut of Special Weapon Assets — embedded heavy weapons (anti-tank guns, machine guns, mortars) attached to infantry units. 8 scenarios from the North African campaign (Halfaya Pass, Gazala, the relief of Tobruk).

## Operation Overlord (2008)

Not a scenario pack but a multiplayer format kit supporting up to 8 players (a commander-in-chief plus field generals per side) on two side-by-side boards forming a 26-hex-wide battlefield. Contains an updated Overlord rules booklet (also covering Eastern Front, Pacific, and Air Pack play), two redesigned 64-card command decks built for the chain-of-command structure, 178 cardboard tokens to stand in for missing army figures, and 8 extra battle dice. No scenarios included — designed to be used with the Battle Map series.

## Battle Map series, series 1 (2008–2010)

Oversized pre-printed paper maps with terrain, obstacles, and unit starting positions already printed on them — set-up in minutes instead of laying out tiles. Each volume includes its scenario rules, summary cards, and historical background, and several include extra figures or cards. Volumes: Vol. 1 *Hedgerow Hell* (Normandy bocage, with extra tank destroyers), Vol. 2 *Tigers in the Snow* (with Tiger tank miniatures), Vol. 3 *Sword of Stalingrad* (introducing Urban Combat cards), Vol. 4 *Disaster at Dieppe* (the 1942 raid).

## Campaign Book Volume 1 (2009)

A hardbound book with more than 50 scenarios organized into linked campaigns (the Normandy campaign, the 1940 Blitzkrieg in the west, and Operation Barbarossa) plus advanced campaign rules where the outcome of one battle carries into the next. Some scenarios require the Terrain Pack and Eastern Front. Later out of print and distributed as a PDF.

## Breakthrough Kit (2010)

Two double-sided, nine-panel oversize board maps covering all four environments (countryside, beach, winter, desert), each 13 × 17 hexes deep — about twice the depth of the standard board — enabling the "Breakthrough" scenario format with deeper defenses and longer battles. Includes 15 scenarios written for the format across the Western, Eastern, Mediterranean, and Pacific theaters.

## Winter Wars (2010)

A complete Ardennes/Bulge-themed expansion: 88 double-sided winter terrain tiles, 28 special-unit badges, 20 round markers, and 16 obstacles, plus new troop types (tank destroyers, heavy anti-tank guns, late-war anti-tank gun, mortar, and machine-gun pieces). Introduces 20 Winter Combat cards (extending the urban-combat-card concept) and 80 command cards redesigned for Breakthrough-format battles. Ten scenarios: six standard ones playable with just the base game, and four giant Breakthrough renditions of the Battle of the Bulge requiring the Eastern Front and Breakthrough Kit.

## Campaign Book Volume 2 (2011)

Second hardbound campaign book: 46 scenarios in 11 campaigns, extending the campaign system with new mechanics (reserves, setbacks, and the like). Requires the Terrain Pack, Pacific Theater, and Air Pack for full use.

## Equipment Pack (2012)

The largest figure set ever released for the game — 186 plastic miniatures filling a base-game-sized box, mostly replacing badge tokens with physical models and adding minor nations: 48 French infantry, 24 Finnish ski troops, 24 Polish cavalry, 12 Italian artillery; four new artillery types (88 mm flak guns, "Long Tom" big guns, M7 Priest mobile artillery, Nebelwerfer rocket launchers); beach-landing equipment (6 landing craft, 6 Hobart's Funnies engineer tanks); three armor types (Elefant tank destroyers, Sd.Kfz 250 half-tracks, Tiger tanks); three vehicle types (Kübelwagen command cars, Willys jeeps, Dodge supply trucks); machine-gun and mortar special-weapon figures; and 6 snipers. Includes a rules booklet, 25 summary cards, and a scenario booklet with 11 standard, 4 Overlord, and 2 Breakthrough battles.

## D-Day Landings (2014)

The largest maps ever produced for the game: six pre-printed Extended Breakthrough battle maps, each 13 × 23 hexes, depicting American Airborne, Utah, Omaha, Gold, Juno, and Sword. Maps can be played singly, joined as double- or triple-map battles, or all six linked into one giant recreation of the entire Normandy coast. Adds reinforcement and terrain-control rules and new supported infantry/armor troop types.

## Battle Map series 2 (2016–2018)

Second series of pre-printed battle maps with extra material: *The Battles of Khalkhin-Gol* (2016) — a six-scenario linked campaign on the 1939 Soviet-Japanese border conflict, plus Breakthrough and Overlord scenarios; *Through Jungle and Desert* (2018) — two new Combat card decks (jungle and desert), 6 standard and 2 Overlord pre-printed scenarios, covering Burma and the first US-German tank clash in North Africa.

## New Flight Plan (2019)

A from-scratch replacement for the discontinued Air Pack with streamlined air-to-air and air-to-ground rules. Contains 16 unpainted airplane miniatures, 3 airplane stands, 30 air combat cards, 8 airplane reference cards, 15 nation markers, 18 machine-gun and 12 bomb markers, a rules booklet, and a scenario booklet with 21 battles. Compatible with all previously published scenarios.

## Campaign Bag

An olive-drab branded carrying bag with pockets sized to hold two copies of the base game plus all expansions; bundled with an exclusive double-sided paper Breakthrough map (open countryside on one face, a unique printed Breakthrough scenario on the other).

## Tactics & Strategy Guide

A separately published strategy book (authored by a community writer rather than the game's designer) analyzing 139 official scenarios with annotated example games, aimed at improving play rather than adding components.
