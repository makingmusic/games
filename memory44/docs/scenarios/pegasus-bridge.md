# Pegasus Bridge — design note

Recorded 06 June 1944 historically; map authored 06 September 2026.

## Design goal

A teaching battle about surprise and two crossings. The attacker has a fat hand
and must rush two bridges before the garrison recovers orders.

## Historical sources (public-domain facts)

- The Caen Canal and Orne River sit a short distance apart east of Ouistreham.
- British glider infantry seized the two bridges in the first minutes of 06 June 1944.
- The garrison was small and unprepared.

## Original layout choices

Blank 13×9. One canal across row 4 with bridges at D4 and J4 so left-centre and
centre-right cards both matter. Towns and woods on the north bank; nine Allied
infantry on the south; six Axis infantry in the towns. No official setup map was
used.

## Parameters (from research notes)

Allies 6 cards, Axis 2 ramping to 4, Allies first, 4 medals, two temporary
Allied bridge medals, infantry only.
