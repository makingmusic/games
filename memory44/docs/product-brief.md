# Product brief

## Working title

**Signal & Steel**

The public identity must remain distinct from existing tabletop brands. The
folder name `memory44` is an internal development label only.

## Promise

A brisk, two-player tactics game about reading terrain, coordinating a small
mixed unit force, and holding a communications relay under pressure.

## First playable: Broken Relay

Two rival expeditionary forces enter a flooded valley from opposite sides.
Each turn, a commander issues two orders to two different units. An order moves
a unit or attacks an enemy. The first side to hold the central relay through two
of its own command phases wins; eliminating the opposing force also wins.

The prototype deliberately avoids a card-driven command system. Later command
mechanics should be designed from first principles and playtested for their own
identity.

## Unit roster

| Unit | Role | Health | Move | Range | Power |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Line | durable close-range unit | 3 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| Rover | fast flanking unit | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 |
| Mortar | fragile ranged support | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 |

Grove terrain costs two movement and reduces incoming damage by one, to a
minimum of one. Ridge terrain adds one damage to attacks made from it. Water is
impassable.

## MVP after this slice

1. Improve movement previews and combat feedback.
2. Add reconnectable room seats and a lobby ready state.
3. Add two more independently designed maps and scenario goals.
4. Add an original command-pressure system after paper prototyping.
5. Add persistence, accessibility settings, and an onboarding match.

## Explicit non-goals

- Reproducing any Memoir '44 scenario, deck, text, artwork, or presentation.
- Advertising compatibility with another game.
- Monetisation before identity, rules, and content receive an IP review.
- Ranked matchmaking before the deterministic engine has broad test coverage.
